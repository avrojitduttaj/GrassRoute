# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in GrassRoute a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We value contributions that are constructive, curious, and considerate. Participants should be able to engage in project discussions, reviews, events, and other community spaces without fear of harassment or discrimination.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, patient, and welcoming.
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback gracefully.
- Critiquing ideas and code rather than attacking people.
- Considering different perspectives and levels of experience.
- Respecting privacy and avoiding the publication of personal information.
- Staying focused on the project and helping keep discussions useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Personal attacks, insults, trolling, or deliberate disruption of discussions.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Stalking, persistent unwanted contact, or encouraging others to target a participant.
- Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional community setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code reviews, discussions, community channels managed by the project, and public interactions where someone is representing GrassRoute or its community.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the project maintainer through the maintainer's GitHub profile: <https://github.com/avrojitduttaj>. Please include links or context where available, while avoiding unnecessary disclosure of sensitive personal information.

Do not use a public issue to share security vulnerabilities, credentials, or private details. If you need to report a safety or security concern, use a private contact channel listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports as fairly and promptly as they can. They will respect the privacy of the people involved to the extent possible, investigate the relevant context, and choose a proportionate response. Maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove someone from project spaces when necessary.

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
